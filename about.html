About MovieHub

Who We Are

MovieHub is a movie discovery website created using HTML, CSS, and Bootstrap. It helps users explore popular movies across different genres such as Action, Sci-Fi, Romance, and Comedy.

Our Mission

Our mission is to provide movie lovers with an easy and attractive platform to discover films, watch trailers, and explore movie categories.

Features

  • Popular Movie Listings
  • Movie Categories
  • Trailer Links
  • Movie Request Form
  • Responsive Bootstrap Design
